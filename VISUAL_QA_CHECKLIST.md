# Visual QA checklist for Kym Gfx

## Desktop QA

- Header and navigation
  - Logo stays aligned and readable at all common desktop widths.
  - Navigation remains uncluttered and intentionally spaced.
  - Primary CTA is visually dominant without overpowering the brand.
  - Hover states are obvious and consistent across links and buttons.

- Hero section
  - Headline retains strong hierarchy and balanced spacing.
  - Portrait, copy, and CTA grouping feel centered and premium.
  - Primary action is clearly the most important choice.
  - Secondary action remains visible without competing with the primary action.

- Portfolio grid
  - Cards maintain consistent height, spacing, and alignment.
  - Filter buttons clearly indicate the active state.
  - Project titles and metadata remain readable at standard browser zoom.
  - Hover feedback feels polished and not overly aggressive.

- Contact and booking actions
  - Email CTA and book CTA are clearly discoverable.
  - Quick contact dock does not obscure key content.
  - Buttons remain easy to identify without feeling visually heavy.

## Mobile QA

- Header and menu
  - Menu toggle is thumb-friendly and easy to hit.
  - Navigation opens and closes cleanly without layout shifts.
  - Header remains balanced and not crowded.

- Hero section
  - Headline wraps cleanly without awkward breaks or overflow.
  - Buttons stack or resize gracefully depending on screen width.
  - Text and portrait remain properly aligned at common mobile widths.
  - Main CTA remains visible without excessive scrolling.

- Portfolio section
  - Cards remain readable and visually consistent on narrow screens.
  - Filter controls are easy to tap and not too close to other UI.
  - Project metadata remains legible without crowding.

- Contact and conversion flow
  - Email and booking buttons are easy to tap.
  - Contact dock does not cover important content on smaller screens.
  - Mailto and booking actions open correctly from mobile browsers.

## Accessibility and polish

- All interactive elements have a clear focus style.
- Text contrast remains strong on both light and dark surfaces.
- No text is clipped, truncated, or pushed outside its container.
- Motion and transitions remain smooth and intentional.
- Buttons read like actions, not decorative elements.

## Conversion CTA checks

- Primary CTA is the most prominent action: "Book a discovery call".
- Secondary CTA is a low-friction action: "Email your brief".
- CTA language is specific, outcome-oriented, and easy to understand.
- Visitor path is clear: browse work -> decide -> book or email.
- Supporting links do not dilute the main conversion intent.

## Recommended review flow

1. Open the homepage on desktop and confirm the hero CTA hierarchy is obvious.
2. Check the same flow on a mobile device or responsive emulator.
3. Verify the email and booking actions are the most prominent contact prompts.
4. Confirm that portfolio and service sections naturally lead into conversion actions.
5. Make sure the site still feels premium, simple, and focused after the CTA changes.
