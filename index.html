A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we
get started.

Every project starts with a conversation.
Here are a few things you might want to know.

Ask me anything
Which design services does Kym Gfx offer?

Kym Gfx is the creative practice of Festus Kimani in Nairobi, Kenya. I offer brand identity, social and event campaigns, motion graphics and video, website design, and ongoing design support for clients worldwide.

How do I book a discovery call?

Request a call, choose Google Meet or Zoom, and share a preferred time and timezone. I’ll confirm availability by email and send the meeting link. A request does not reserve a time.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the scope, deliverables, and timeline. Share your brief and budget, and I’ll put together a quote before we start.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Nairobi, Kenya (GMT+3), and work remotely with clients worldwide. We can coordinate feedback and project updates across time zones.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Yes. Share your brand guidelines and existing assets so I can build new work that feels consistent with your identity.

Do you take on ongoing design work?

Yes. For recurring social content, event flyers, and campaigns, we can agree on a monthly scope and a delivery schedule that fits your needs.

Are you open to a remote design role?

Yes. I’m open to worldwide remote design opportunities. Email the role details, location eligibility, and team information, and I’ll take a look.
